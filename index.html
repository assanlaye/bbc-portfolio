01 · Mobile UI · Figma

RideGambia

Ride-booking UI concept for Gambian commuters.

A self-initiated 4-screen flow: Welcome, Sign up, Set route, Choose ride. Uses +220 phone formatting, area-based sign-up, and fares in dalasi. Driver cards show car, rating, plate, and arrival time — with driver contact hidden until the ride is confirmed.

Nopal Nit

Sending money should take seconds, not menus.

A self-initiated concept for sending money quickly and clearly. The flow covers Home, Send Money, and a receipt-style Success screen. A balance card and three quick actions keep home simple; preset amount chips and a custom keypad make sending fast; the confirmation shows transaction ID, fees, and total.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

Graphic design for flyers, posters, menus, and social ads; logo and brand mark exploration; presentation design; and early mobile UI/UX concepts as I grow that practice.

How can we start a project together?

Email me with your goals, timeline, and any references. We’ll clarify the brief, agree on deliverables, and move into concepts.

Can you help with content or branding too?

Yes — I can support visual identity pieces, promotional messaging layouts, and on-brand social/print materials alongside core design work.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. I’m based in The Gambia and collaborate remotely via email and shared files.

Can you redesign my existing flyer or website visuals?

Yes. Share what you have today and the outcome you want — I’ll rebuild the visual system for clarity, hierarchy, and stronger brand presence.

What is your typical project timeline?

Simple flyers and social graphics often turn around quickly; branding and multi-screen UI concepts take longer depending on feedback rounds. I’ll share a clear estimate before we start.
